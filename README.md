# Opus Care Support Services

Foundation website for **Opus Care Support Services**, a proposed NSW disability support business.

## Current positioning

- Initial model: unregistered NDIS provider
- Intended audience: self-managed and plan-managed NDIS participants
- Service area: Ballina through the Northern Rivers and Clarence Valley to Grafton and Coffs Harbour
- Current website content is a development foundation and must not be treated as final business, legal, insurance or registration information.

## Included

- Responsive marketing website
- Opus Care visual identity and starter logo mark
- Service overview
- Participant/professional referral form UI
- FAQ and contact sections
- Draft Privacy & Confidentiality page
- Draft Complaints & Feedback page
- Draft Incident Management page
- Draft NDIS Code of Conduct commitment

## Before public launch

Confirm and replace placeholders for:

- Legal entity / sole trader name and ABN
- Registered business name
- Domain and email addresses
- Phone number and service locations
- Insurance coverage and wording
- Final service scope and exclusions
- Provider registration status
- Current NDIS support items and pricing
- Secure form submission and record storage
- Privacy, consent, retention and security controls
- Complaints and incident escalation contacts
- Accessibility testing and legal review
- Authentic business photography and final brand assets
